How MedConnect Operates

1. Patient Inquires

A patient uploads a prescription or enters a specific medicine name along with their location.

2. Geographic Radius Filter

The system queries verified pharmacies within 5 km using the Haversine formula, with single-click +5 km radius expansion.

3. Direct Pharmacy Response

Pharmacies respond with All Available, Some Available, or Not Available, and contact coordinates.
